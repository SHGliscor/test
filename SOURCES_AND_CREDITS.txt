v0.16 live-seed Auto RNG reference / comparison
- Pokémon Automation FRLG RNG programs and documentation by Astro (Tom) and contributors
  were used as a behavioral reference for automatic calibration on Switch FRLG.
  https://pokemonautomation.github.io/Programs/PokemonFRLG/RngManipulationGuide.html
- v0.16 does not copy the controller-only seed-timer approach. Its experimental path
  uses this project's existing direct live 32-bit PokeRNG RAM read, searches forward
  from the observed state, and self-calibrates controller trigger lead from validated PK3 results.
- RAM remains read-only; v0.16 never writes, freezes, or forces the RNG state.
